Saltline is a round-based tactical FPS built with three.js. Two teams of three fight at a golden-hour salt-harvest terminal: the Jackals carry a charge they must plant at site A or B, the Wardens hold the lanes and defuse it. Rounds run on an economy — win, loss, and kill money buys rifles, armour, and frag and smoke grenades from the buy menu — and the bots run routes, hold positions, and react to what they see and hear. First to 5 rounds takes the match.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8000 in a desktop browser. Click Play, and the game takes pointer lock.

## Controls

WASD move · mouse look · left click fire · right click aim · R reload · 1/2 or wheel weapons · G frag grenade · F smoke grenade · B buy menu · E (hold) plant/defuse · Space jump · C or Ctrl crouch · Shift walk

On touch screens: left-thumb analog stick to move, drag the right side to look, and on-screen buttons for fire, aim, jump, crouch, reload, weapon swap, grenades, use, and buy.
